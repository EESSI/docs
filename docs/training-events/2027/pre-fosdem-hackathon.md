# Pre-FOSDEM EESSI hackathon (2027)

An EESSI hackathon is being organized ([again](../../blog/posts/2026/02/FOSDEM-2026.md)) in Brussels,
right before the <a href="https://fosdem.org/2027" target="_blank">FOSDEM'27</a> weekend.

No schedule, just hands-on collaborating on something related to EESSI.

## Practical info

- Dates: **Thu+Fri 28-29 January 2027** (09:00-18:00)
- Location: <a href="https://hoek38.be">Hoek 38</a>, Brussels (Belgium)

## Registration

Attending the hackathon is free, but registration is required for practical reasons.

Register via <a href="https://event.ugent.be/registration/prefosdemeessihackathon" target="_blank">https://event.ugent.be/registration/prefosdemeessihackathon</a>.

We have about 20 seats available.
